Privacy Policy

Day & Night: Hex Kingdoms — by Melton Games · Last updated: 24 November 2026

In short: the game has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Your saved games stay on your device. The only information that ever leaves your device is a player name and score, and only if you choose to submit a win to the online leaderboard.

Who we are

“Day & Night: Hex Kingdoms” (the “game”) is published by Melton Games (“we”, “us”). This policy applies to the Android app (package com.jayet95.heroesjs) and to the web version of the game.

What is stored on your device

The game keeps the following only on your device (browser or app storage). We never receive it:

  • your saved games and autosave;
  • your settings (volume, interface size, hand preference, etc.);
  • achievements and your local best-score list;
  • a cache of the game files so it can work offline.

Clearing the app data or browser data, or uninstalling the app, removes all of this.

What is sent to us

Only if you finish a game and submit your score to the online leaderboard:

DataWhy
The player name you typeshown on the leaderboard
Score, day count, difficulty, map size, castle and hero nameranking and filtering
Your IP address (processed by our hosting provider, Cloudflare)to limit submissions to one per minute and stop abuse; it is used for this temporary check, held for about a minute and is not shown or linked to your name

Please do not enter your real name or any personal information as a player name — the leaderboard is public. You can play without ever submitting a score.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data.
  • We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs, and we do not sell or share personal data.
  • We do not require an account or sign-in.
  • The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

Third parties

The web version and the leaderboard are hosted on Cloudflare, which processes technical request data (such as IP address) as described in its own privacy policy. The Android app itself contains no third-party advertising or analytics code.

Your choices and rights

  • Do not submit scores, and nothing is sent to us.
  • To have a leaderboard entry removed, contact us (below) with the name and score shown.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete data about you. Because the only data we hold is a public leaderboard entry, a removal request covers it.

Changes

If we change this policy (for example if ads are added in the future), we will update this page and the date above, and the app’s store listing and data-safety information will be updated to match.
